# 2026-27 season projection (current rosters)

- 10,000 simulated seasons, seed 2027, every team starts 0-0.
- Strength = expected net rating per 100 possessions vs an average team.
- Range = 10th to 90th percentile of simulated wins.
- Playoff = made the playoffs (seeds 1-6, or won through the play-in). Play-in = finished 7-10.
- Seeds use the NBA's tiebreakers; #1 seed = regular-season first place.
- Long-term injury adjusted (column, or + in the terminal): teams with a player in config/long_injuries.json (as of 2026-11-09): his availability is set to expected games played / 82.
- Monte Carlo SE (largest across teams): avg wins 0.10, playoff 0.5 pts, play-in 0.5 pts, #1 seed 0.5 pts. Per-team values are in the CSV.
